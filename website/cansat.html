01 / MEASURE, GUIDE, RECOVER

A mission with a fallback.

Temperature and pressure come first. Steering is an experiment that must earn its place through measured tests.

01

Measure

Record pressure, temperature, motion and position. Send the primary atmospheric measurements to the ground at least once per second.

02

Guide

Combine GPS target error with measured orientation and rotation. Adjust the four airbrakes in the rotating body frame during qualified descent.

03

Recover

A separate ESP32-S3 supervises commands and owns the servo outputs. The passive parachute provides recovery even without active guidance.

How does steering work while the CanSat rotates?

The controller transforms the direction towards the GPS target into the CanSat's current body orientation, allowing for rotation during servo delay. It then allocates panel movement using measured aerodynamic response. GPS travel direction is not used as the heading.

What happens if an airbrake jams?

If its physical angle is known, the opposite panel moves towards the same angle and the perpendicular pair remains available. Missing feedback, multiple failures or poor control response stop precision guidance. Working panels move to a tested safe setting; maximum deployment stays disabled until verified.

What if the main flight program stops?

The guardian runs on a separate board. It checks fresh commands, target consistency and expected versus measured movement. A safety trip latches guidance off. Recovery and primary data take priority over landing accuracy.

What has been tested so far?

The Python control core has passed 22 host test methods, including rotation, actuator faults, corrupt packets and a simulated main-task freeze. Sensor drivers, board timing, aerodynamic calibration and physical drop tests remain outstanding. The hardware has not yet been purchased.

CanSat Components

The ESP32-S3 is our selected controller family. Other parts are candidates or prototype equipment until their integration tests are complete.

SELECTED PLATFORM

ESP32S3MicroPython · two physical boards

ESP32-S3 controllers

One board runs guidance; the other supervises it and controls the servos. The DevKitC-1 N8R8 is our recommended bench model, with 8 MB flash and 8 MB PSRAM. The flight assembly still needs fit and power checks.

Board specifications ↗

BMP390 Barometric Sensor

The BMP390 pressure sensor supports altitude and pressure measurements during testing and flight, giving the team data for descent profile analysis.

Motion and heading

The pictured ADXL335 is an early acceleration-sensor option. Guidance needs a heading-capable IMU and gyroscope; the BNO085 is a candidate. An accelerometer alone cannot provide the required orientation.

LoRa telemetry

An external LoRa link will carry atmospheric measurements and flight status to the ground. The exact radio variant, frequency and event configuration are still to be confirmed.

Airbrake actuation

The pictured SG90 is an early prototype option; MG90S-class servos are also under consideration. Four servos need measured torque and power margins, with separate hinge feedback to detect actual panel movement.

Fabrication and Filaments

The printer, Automatic Material System hardware, and engineering filaments are used to prototype, test, and refine the physical CanSat structure.

Bambu Lab X2D 3D Printer

The X2D printer manufactures the CanSat structure, fixtures, test pieces, and revised CAD iterations. Fast printing helps the team test fit and strength before finalising a design.

Automatic Material System

The AMS 2 Pro and two AMS HT dryers are part of the fabrication process. They manage multi-material printing and keep engineering filaments dry for consistent structural parts.

ASA Aero

ASA Aero is used for lightweight structural printing where the team wants a stronger engineering material while keeping printed parts efficient.

TPU 90A

TPU 90A gives the team a flexible material option for shock-absorbing, compliant, or grip-like parts during mechanism and fit testing.

PETG CF

PETG CF is used for carbon-fibre-reinforced parts where stiffness, dimensional stability, and a clean engineering finish are useful.

PLA HS

PLA HS is a high-speed PLA used for rapid prototyping, letting the team print early CAD iterations quickly before moving to specialist materials.

Build Workflow

The project moves from model to print to electronics integration, with notebook evidence collected along the way.

1

Design

CAD defines the CanSat body, internal mounts, access points, and recovery interfaces.

2

Print

The X2D, AMS 2 Pro, and AMS HT dryers produce prototypes and final parts with controlled PLA HS, ASA Aero, TPU 90A, and PETG CF handling.

3

Integrate

The ESP32-S3 boards, sensors and actuators are fitted and wired, then the Python software is tested against real measurements and injected faults.

4

Document

Testing, failures, changes, and final decisions are recorded in the engineering notebook.
